Stick0ne – App Concept and Main Features
Stick0ne is a simple and creative app that allows users to place their favorite stickers on their phone’s home screen and lock screen. Users can search for existing stickers or create their own, customize their position and transparency, and save their designs.
5 Main Features
1. Sticker Search: A dedicated navigation section where users can search for and explore the available stickers.

2. Create Your Own Sticker: A separate navigation section that allows users to create their own custom stickers.

3. Sticker Placement and Customization: When a user selects or creates a sticker, a blank setup screen appears with the selected sticker. Users can move the sticker anywhere on the screen and adjust its transparency to achieve their desired look.

4. In-App Editing Only: Stickers should not be movable or interactive outside the app. Users must open Stick0ne to edit, reposition, adjust the transparency, or change their stickers. Once the setup is complete, the stickers become fixed in place.

5. Home Screen and Lock Screen Display: After completing the setup, users can display their customized stickers on both their phone's home screen and lock screen.S

Overall Goal: Stick0ne aims to provide a simple, fun, and user-friendly experience that allows users to personalize their phone screens with custom stickers while maintaining full control over their placement and appearance.
